Privacy Policy

Property Claims Intake Software — [YOUR COMPANY NAME]
Effective Date: May 11, 2026
This Privacy Policy describes how [YOUR COMPANY NAME] ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information in connection with our claims intake software platform (the "Platform"). This policy applies to all users of the Platform, including insurance carrier clients ("Carriers"), their employees and adjusters ("Authorized Users"), and policyholders who submit claim information through the Platform ("Policyholders").

By accessing or using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information Collected from Policyholders

When a Policyholder submits a claim through the Platform, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact information — including first and last name, telephone number, and email address
  • Property information — including street address, city, state, and ZIP code
  • Policy information — including policy number and date of loss
  • Loss description — including a free-text description of the damage
  • Damage details — including loss type, affected areas, and contents information
  • Contractor and vendor information — including company names, contact persons, and phone numbers
  • Uploaded documents — including photographs, invoices, estimates, and inspection reports

1.2 Information Collected from Authorized Users

When Carriers and their Authorized Users access the Platform, we collect:

  • Account credentials — including email address and encrypted password
  • Role and organizational information — including adjuster role, carrier affiliation, and access permissions
  • Activity data — including login timestamps, claims accessed, and actions taken within the Platform

1.3 Automatically Collected Information

The Platform automatically collects certain technical information when you access it, including IP address, browser type, device type, and session duration. This information is used for security monitoring and platform performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected through the Platform solely for the following purposes:

  • To facilitate the submission, processing, and triage of property insurance claims
  • To generate AI-assisted severity assessments and next-best-action recommendations for Authorized Users
  • To verify weather data against reported dates and locations of loss
  • To enable Carriers and their Authorized Users to review, manage, and act on submitted claims
  • To authenticate users and maintain the security of the Platform
  • To provide technical support and maintain platform functionality
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or share Policyholder information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. We do not use Policyholder claim data to train artificial intelligence models without explicit written consent from the applicable Carrier.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

The information processed through the Platform constitutes Non-Public Personal Information (NPI) as defined under the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA). We process this information as a service provider to Carrier clients, who are the financial institutions responsible for GLBA compliance with respect to their policyholders. Our processing is conducted solely as directed by and on behalf of each Carrier pursuant to applicable service agreements.

4. Data Isolation and Multi-Tenancy

Each Carrier client operates within a fully isolated data environment. Row-level security controls ensure that claim data, user accounts, and documents belonging to one Carrier are never accessible to users of another Carrier. Authorized Users may only access claims and data associated with their own organization.

5. Data Storage and Security

All data stored in the Platform is encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption. All data transmitted between users and the Platform is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3. We use Supabase as our database and storage provider, which maintains SOC 2 Type II certification and industry-standard security controls.

Uploaded documents and photographs are stored in encrypted cloud storage with access controls that restrict retrieval to Authorized Users of the associated Carrier.

We maintain an Information Security Program consistent with the requirements of the GLBA Safeguards Rule and the NAIC Insurance Data Security Model Law.

6. Data Retention

Claim data and associated documents are retained for the period specified in each Carrier's service agreement, or as required by applicable state insurance regulations. Upon termination of a Carrier's service agreement, we will provide a complete export of that Carrier's data and permanently delete it from our systems within 30 days unless a longer retention period is required by law.

7. Third-Party Service Providers

We engage the following categories of sub-processors to operate the Platform:

  • Database and storage infrastructure — Supabase
  • Website hosting and content delivery — Netlify
  • Weather data verification — third-party weather API services
  • Email notification services — third-party transactional email providers

All sub-processors are required to maintain confidentiality obligations and security standards consistent with this Privacy Policy and applicable law.

8. Your Rights

Policyholders have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information submitted through the Platform. Such requests should be submitted to the Carrier through which the claim was filed, as the Carrier is the primary data controller under GLBA.

Authorized Users may update their account information by contacting their Carrier administrator or by emailing us at [YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS].

9. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a security breach affecting Policyholder NPI, we will notify affected Carriers within 72 hours of discovery, consistent with the notification requirements of the NAIC Insurance Data Security Model Law and applicable state law. Carriers are responsible for notifying their policyholders as required by applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify Carrier clients of material changes via email at least 30 days before the changes take effect. Continued use of the Platform after the effective date of any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
